隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

放松舱无需账号，玩具、白噪音和呼吸练习可以离线使用。1.9.0 及更早的 iOS 版本没有使用统计。

从 iOS 1.10.0 起，匿名使用统计默认关闭。只有在首页「隐私与改进」主动开启后，应用才会向我们在腾讯云 CloudBase 上海区的统计服务发送事件，用于了解功能使用情况和发现运行问题。

事件仅包括：同意统计、首次同意后打开应用、打开应用、查看首页、查看隐私设置、发生错误。随事件发送随机生成的安装标识和事件编号、应用标识、版本、平台和时间；打开事件还可包含距首次同意打开的天数。安装标识可关联同一次安装的事件，不是广告标识。我们不使用这些事件做广告或跨应用追踪。

不会发送呼吸记录、玩具收藏、搜索词、混音或触感设置、错误消息或堆栈。练习记录和偏好保存在设备上。你可以随时在同一入口关闭统计；关闭后停止发送，并清除本地安装标识、待发送事件和发送记录。关闭统计不能撤回已经送达的事件。

隐私咨询或删除请求，请联系 kobe60843@gmail.com。

ChillPod needs no account. Toys, soundscapes and breathing exercises work offline. iOS version 1.9.0 and earlier have no usage analytics.

Starting in iOS 1.10.0, anonymous usage statistics are off by default. Only if you turn them on in Privacy & improvements on the home screen does ChillPod send events to our analytics service on Tencent CloudBase in Shanghai, to understand feature use and identify reliability issues.

Events are limited to consent, first open after consent, app opens, home views, privacy settings views and the occurrence of an error. Each includes random installation and event IDs, app ID, version, platform and timestamp. App opens may include days since first open after consent. The installation ID groups events from one installation; it is not an advertising ID. We do not use these events for advertising or cross-app tracking.

We do not send breathing records, favorites, searches, mix or haptic settings, error messages or stack traces. Practice history and preferences stay on your device. Turning statistics off stops delivery and clears the local installation ID, pending events and delivery history. It cannot recall events already delivered.

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact kobe60843@gmail.com.
