隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

放松舱不收集任何数据。

  • 无账号系统，无需注册或登录
  • 不收集、不存储、不传输任何个人信息或设备信息
  • 无第三方 SDK、无广告、无统计分析
  • 呼吸练习记录仅保存在你的设备本地，删除 App 即清除
  • App 完全离线运行，不发起任何网络请求

ChillPod collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no ads and no third-party SDK. The app works fully offline and never makes a network request. Breathing practice history is stored only on your device and is removed when you delete the app.
