ChillPod Android Privacy Policy

Effective and updated: October 26, 2026. Android version 0.23.0.

ChillPod Android requires no registration and includes no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK. The app has no Internet permission and does not upload personal information to developer servers.

Personal sound mixes, favorites, wooden-fish tap counts, completed practice records and review-prompt counters are stored only on your device. System backup is disabled. Uninstalling the app deletes this local data. The app does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Notification permission provides soundscape playback controls. A foreground media service and wake lock support background playback. Vibration permission provides tactile feedback. Declining notification permission does not affect the other relaxation features.

The Android app includes the Google Play in-app review component. Only versions installed from Google Play may request a skippable store review prompt after a practice session. The store service decides whether to show it and processes reviews under its own privacy policy. ChillPod itself does not receive your review account information. Other installation sources do not trigger this flow.

This version has no account, cloud sync, purchases or subscriptions. All features are accessible without login. The developer does not collect personal information from children or other users through this app. ChillPod is a relaxation tool, not a medical diagnosis or treatment service.
